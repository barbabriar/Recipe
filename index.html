EGG FRIED RICE RECIPE!

want to learn how to make a yummy fried rice? this is the right place!!! :3 :3 :3 filler text

Time required:

30 minutes with premade rice

Ingredients:

  • Cooking oil
  • Rice
  • Egg
  • Carrots
  • Corn
  • Peas
  • Green Onions
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Soy sauce
  • Vinegar
  • Any additional ingredients
Feel free to swap any ingredients out and adjust the ratios to your liking!

Process:

  1. Prepare all ingredients:
    • Beat the eggs
    • Cut green onions and seperate the green and white parts into two piles
    • Dice carrots
    • Set aside the desired amount of corn and peas
    • Cut garlic
  2. Turn stove to full heat, and Add oil to pan
  3. When the pan is hot, add in the white part of the green onions. Cook until fragrant
  4. Add the carrots, and cook until it is mostly cooked
  5. Add in corn and peas. Cook until mixture is fully cooked
  6. Take it out of the pan and set aside
  7. add oil
  8. add eggs, cook and scramble until desired consistency is achieved
  9. Remove eggs from pan and set aside
  10. add oil
  11. add garlic. cook until fragrant
  12. add rice
  13. add egg. cook for a while
  14. add mixture of vegetables
  15. add seasoning as desired
  16. let dish cool and enjoy!
