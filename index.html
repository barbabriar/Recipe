EGG FRIED RICE RECIPE!

Fried rice is a versatile dish that can be made to suit various tastes and occasions. With this recipe, you can learn to make a simple and delicious fried rice!

(Image source)

Time required:

~25 minutes

Ingredients:

  • Cooking oil
  • 1 cup of cooked rice
  • 3 eggs
  • 1/2 cup of mixed vegetable:
    • Corn
    • Carrots
    • Peas
    • Feel free to switch these up!
  • Optional: green onions
  • Optional: garlic
  • Seasoning:
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Soy sauce
    • Vinegar
    • Seasoning powder
Feel free to swap any ingredients out and adjust the ratios to your liking!

Process:

  1. Prepare all ingredients:
    • Beat the eggs
    • Cut green onions and seperate the green and white parts
    • Set aside the desired amount of mixed vegetables
    • Cut garlic
  2. Turn stove to full heat, and add cooking oil to the pan. Once heated, add the white part of green onions. Cook until fragrant.
  3. Add in mixed vegetables, and stir until it is fully cooked. Set aside
  4. Add oil to the pan. Once heated, add eggs and scramble until half cooked. Set aside
  5. Add oil. Once heated, add garlic and cook until fragrant
  6. Add rice to the pan. Scramble for a while, and ensure that there are no chunks
  7. Add egg. Stir until the egg is integrated with the rice
  8. Add mixture of vegetables. Stir until the vegetables are integrated with the egg and rice
  9. Add seasoning as desired
  10. Garnish with the remaining green onion
  11. Let dish cool and enjoy!
